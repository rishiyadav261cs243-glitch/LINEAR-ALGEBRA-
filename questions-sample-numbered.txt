# Numbered format (use the same layout in a Word .docx or a .pdf file).
1. The determinant of a triangular matrix is…
A) The sum of the diagonal
B) The product of the diagonal
C) Always 0
D) Always 1
Answer: B

2. If det A = 0 for a square matrix A, then A is…
A) Invertible
B) Singular (not invertible)
C) Orthogonal
D) Symmetric
Answer: B

3. The eigenvalues of [2 0; 0 −3] are…
A) 2 and 3
B) 2 and −3
C) 0 and 1
D) −2 and 3
Answer: B

# Linear Algebra for B.Tech Semester 1

An installable, offline-ready study app for first-semester B.Tech Linear Algebra, covering the standard first-course syllabus (the topic order follows David C. Lay's *Linear Algebra and Its Applications*). All explanations, proofs and problems are written originally for this app.

## Features
- Detailed notes with theorems, full proofs and "notable points"
- Worked examples and a large set of challenging practice problems (tagged Standard, Hard and Proof)
- Chapter tests: 40% quiz and 60% subjective questions, with self-marking
- Progress tracking saved on your device
- Works offline after the first visit
- Installs on your phone with its own icon, no app store needed

## Syllabus
1. Linear equations in linear algebra
2. Matrix algebra
3. Determinants
4. Vector spaces
5. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors
6. Orthogonality and least squares
7. Symmetric matrices and quadratic forms

## Status
- Chapter 1: notes, proofs, worked examples, 50 challenge problems, chapter test
- Chapters 2 to 7: in progress

## How to install on your phone
1. Open the app link in your browser (add your link here once Pages is live).
2. Android (Chrome): tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
4. Open it once with internet. After that it works offline.

## Files
- `index.html`: the app
- `manifest.webmanifest`: app name and icon settings
- `sw.js`: offline support
- `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: app icons
- `problems1.js`: Chapter 1 challenge problems
